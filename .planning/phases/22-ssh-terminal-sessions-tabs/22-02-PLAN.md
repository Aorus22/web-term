  <action>
1. Update `desktop-gpui/crates/webterm/Cargo.toml`:
   - Ensure `webterm-backend-client` and `webterm-terminal` are available.
2. Create `desktop-gpui/crates/webterm/src/session.rs`:
   - `SessionStatus`: `Connecting`, `Connected`, `Reconnecting { attempt: usize, next_retry_secs: usize }`, `Disconnected(Option<String>)`.
   - `TerminalTab`:
     - `id: u64`
     - `session_id: Option<String>`
     - `title: String`
     - `status: SessionStatus`
     - `view: Entity<TerminalView>`
     - `ws_handle: Option<TerminalWsHandle>`
     - `session_type: String` ("local" | "ssh")
     - `connection_id: Option<String>`
   - `TerminalSessionManager`:
     - `tabs: Vec<TerminalTab>`
     - `active_tab_index: usize`
     - `next_tab_id: u64`
     - `open_tab(...)`: creates terminal, connects or initializes transport, hooks input/resize callbacks, and spawns async output reader task pumping bytes into `terminal.lock().process_bytes(&bytes)`.
     - `switch_tab(index)`: sets `active_tab_index` and requests focus.
     - `close_tab(index)`: disconnects ws handle, cleans up tab, adjusts `active_tab_index`.
     - `cycle_next_tab()`: cycles to `(active + 1) % len`.
     - `cycle_prev_tab()`: cycles to previous.
     - `jump_to_tab(index)`: jumps to 0..8 for Alt+1..9.
     - `active_tab()` / `active_tab_mut()`.
3. Integrate `TerminalSessionManager` into `AppState` in `desktop-gpui/crates/webterm/src/app_state.rs`.
  </action>
